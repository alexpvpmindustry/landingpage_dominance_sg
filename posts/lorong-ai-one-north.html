Lorong AI: Blueprint for Singapore's Future AI Park in One-North

Date: February 23, 2026

Singapore has taken a major step toward establishing itself as a premier destination for artificial intelligence innovation with the unveiling of Lorong AI, a collaborative hub that will serve as the blueprint for the nation's first dedicated AI park in the one-north district.

A Vision for AI Excellence

The Lorong AI initiative represents Singapore's commitment to creating a vibrant ecosystem where AI researchers, startups, and established technology companies can collaborate, innovate, and scale their solutions. Located in the heart of the one-north innovation district, the hub is designed to foster cross-pollination between academia and industry.

"Lorong AI is more than just a physical space," said the Minister for Communications and Information. "It's a living laboratory where the best minds in AI can come together to solve complex challenges and create impactful solutions that benefit Singapore and the region."

Key Features of the Hub

The Lorong AI collaborative hub includes several key facilities and programs:

  • Shared Research Facilities: State-of-the-art computing infrastructure including high-performance GPU clusters available to all hub participants
  • Co-working Spaces: Flexible workspaces designed for AI startups and established companies working on AI projects
  • Innovation Labs: Dedicated spaces for industry partners to collaborate with academic researchers on real-world AI applications
  • Talent Development Programs: Training initiatives to develop the next generation of AI talent for Singapore
  • Startup Incubator: Support services including mentorship, funding connections, and business development assistance

Strategic Location in One-North

The choice of one-north as the location for Lorong AI is strategic. The district is already home to several research institutions, including the Agency for Science, Technology and Research (A*STAR), as well as technology companies and universities. This concentration of expertise creates an ideal environment for AI innovation.

By situating the AI hub within this existing innovation ecosystem, Singapore aims to accelerate the translation of AI research into commercial applications. The proximity to academic institutions also facilitates easier collaboration between researchers and industry practitioners.

Industry Response

The announcement of Lorong AI has been welcomed by the technology industry. Several major AI companies have already committed to establishing a presence in the hub, attracted by the combination of world-class infrastructure, talent pool, and government support.

Local startups are also expressing enthusiasm about the opportunities that Lorong AI will provide. "Having access to shared computing resources and the ability to collaborate with researchers and larger companies will be game-changing for us," said the founder of a local AI startup focused on natural language processing.

Part of Singapore's AI Strategy

Lorong AI is the latest addition to Singapore's comprehensive AI strategy, which includes investments in research, talent development, and industry adoption. The hub complements other initiatives such as AI Singapore's research programs and the recently announced Budget 2026 investments in AI infrastructure.

As Singapore works toward its goal of becoming Southeast Asia's premier AI hub, Lorong AI represents a tangible commitment to creating the physical and collaborative infrastructure needed to achieve that ambition.
