Singapore's AI Governance Framework Gets Updated

Date: February 2026

Singapore has unveiled an updated AI Governance Framework, introducing new guidelines for generative AI deployment and stricter requirements for high-risk AI systems. The Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA) announced that companies deploying AI in sensitive sectors must now conduct mandatory impact assessments before launch.

Key updates include:

  • Generative AI Guidelines: New requirements for transparency in AI-generated content
  • Sector-Specific Rules: Enhanced oversight for AI in healthcare, finance, and education
  • Risk Tiers: A three-tier classification system for AI applications
  • Audit Requirements: Annual AI system audits for high-risk deployments

Industry leaders have welcomed the framework, noting that clear regulations will help build public trust while enabling innovation. The Singapore AI Governance Alliance has been expanded to include more small and medium enterprises.
