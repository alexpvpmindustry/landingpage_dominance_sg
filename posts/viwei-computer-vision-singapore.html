In the rapidly evolving landscape of Singapore's AI ecosystem, Vi维智能 (ViWei) has emerged as a standout player in computer vision technology. This homegrown startup is making significant strides in transforming how businesses across Southeast Asia approach retail analytics and security solutions.

From Research Lab to Market Leader

Founded by a team of NUS (National University of Singapore) alumni in 2023, Vi维智能 originated from cutting-edge research in deep learning and neural network architectures specifically designed for real-time video analysis. The company quickly pivoted from pure research to commercial applications, identifying massive opportunities in Singapore's retail and security sectors.

"We saw a gap between the impressive computer vision models published in academic papers and what businesses actually needed," explained Dr. Chen Wei Ming, ViWei's co-founder and CEO. "Our focus has always been on making enterprise-grade AI accessible and practical for real-world deployment."

Revolutionizing Retail Analytics

ViWei's retail analytics platform has become increasingly popular among Singapore's shopping malls and retail chains. The system uses advanced object detection and tracking algorithms to provide real-time insights into customer behavior, foot traffic patterns, and store performance metrics.

Key features of ViWei's retail solution include:

1. Heat Mapping: AI-powered analysis creates detailed heat maps showing which areas of a store attract the most attention, helping retailers optimize store layouts and product placements.

2. Queue Detection: The system automatically alerts staff when checkout queues exceed defined thresholds, enabling faster customer service and improved shopping experiences.

3. Customer Demographics Analysis: Privacy-preserving AI estimates customer demographics (age groups, family sizes) without collecting personal data, providing valuable insights for targeted marketing.

4. Inventory Monitoring: Computer vision monitors shelf stock levels in real-time, automatically triggering restocking alerts when products run low.

Security Applications Beyond Traditional Surveillance

Beyond retail, ViWei has developed sophisticated security applications that go far beyond traditional CCTV surveillance. Their AI security platform can detect unusual behaviors, identify potential safety hazards, and integrate with existing security infrastructure.

The system has been particularly valuable for Singapore's industrial and logistics sectors, where it helps monitor warehouse operations, detect unauthorized access, and ensure workplace safety compliance.

Regional Expansion and Growth

ViWei's success in Singapore has paved the way for regional expansion. The company has already established partnerships with retailers and security firms in Malaysia, Thailand, and Indonesia. Their cloud-based architecture allows for easy deployment across different markets while maintaining data residency compliance.

The startup has raised Series A funding of $8 million in late 2025, led by Sequoia Capital's Southeast Asia arm, with participation from existing investors. The funding will support further R&D and market expansion throughout the region.

Commitment to Responsible AI

Like many Singapore AI companies, ViWei places strong emphasis on responsible AI development. All their systems are designed with privacy by default, processing video locally on edge devices rather than transmitting raw footage to cloud servers. The company has also implemented strict data retention policies and provides transparent reporting on AI decision-making processes.

"Trust is foundational in our industry," noted Dr. Chen. "We work closely with PDPC (Personal Data Protection Commission) guidelines and have built our entire infrastructure around privacy-preserving principles."

What's Next for ViWei

Looking ahead, ViWei is investing heavily in multimodal AI systems that combine visual recognition with natural language processing. This will enable more sophisticated interactive AI assistants for retail environments—imagine walking into a store and having an AI guide you to exactly the products you need.

The company is also exploring applications in Singapore's growing eldercare sector, using computer vision to monitor elderly individuals and detect falls or unusual behaviors in nursing homes and assisted living facilities.
