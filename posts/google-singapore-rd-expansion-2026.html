Google Expands R&D Footprint in Singapore with New Engineering Hub

Date: March 2, 2026

In a significant move that underscores Singapore's growing importance in the global AI landscape, Google has announced a major expansion of its research and development footprint in the city-state. The technology giant revealed plans to scale its teams across software engineering, user experience design, and research science, with a strategic focus on cloud engineering teams.

A New Chapter in Google-Singapore Collaboration

This expansion builds upon the opening of the Google DeepMind Research Lab in Singapore last November, further cementing the city-state's position as a key hub for AI innovation in Asia. Ben King, managing director of Google Singapore, emphasized the company's commitment to building solutions alongside Singapore that address unique local challenges and drive new growth.

"We are growing our engineering and R&D teams in the country—not just to bring Google's technology here, but to also build solutions alongside Singapore that solve its unique challenges and drive new growth," King said at the announcement event on February 10th.

Google Cloud Singapore Engineering Center

At the heart of this expansion is the new Google Cloud Singapore Engineering Center, a hub designed to house engineers and support teams who will partner with firms across multiple industries to solve their most pressing challenges. The center will focus on sectors including robotics and clean energy, reflecting Singapore's strategic priorities in sustainable development and advanced manufacturing.

Moe Abdula, vice-president for customer engineering at Google Cloud Asia-Pacific, highlighted the significance of co-locating software engineers with the DeepMind lab in Singapore. This proximity, he noted, enables Google to "transform research into ready-to-deploy products at rapid speed."

Empowering Singapore's AI Workforce

Beyond infrastructure, Google is making substantial investments in developing Singapore's AI talent pool. The company launched Startup School: Prompt to Prototype, an online training course designed to teach founders how to use Google's Gemini and AI Studio products to transform ideas into AI prototypes.

Google also introduced AI Living Labs in partnership with Singapore's Ministry of Education. These initiatives target young people and educators, providing hands-on workshops and industry collaboration opportunities. The first lab has been launched at the Institute of Technical Education College East, with a second planned for Nanyang Polytechnic. The facilities aim to reach 50,000 Singaporeans by 2027.

Skills Ignition SG: Training the Next Generation

In collaboration with the Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA), Google is also rolling out the Skills Ignition SG AI Challenge. This initiative aims to train 500 graduates and mid-career professionals in AI skills through a two-month accelerator programme. Participants will learn to design AI workflows for sectors including accounting, human resources, and legal services.

Minister for Digital Development and Information Josephine Teo expressed her support for the initiative, stating: "We believe very much that whatever it is that we create in Singapore goes so much further when others get a chance to benefit from them."

Building AI Infrastructure for Southeast Asia

To further strengthen regional AI capabilities, Google.org, the company's philanthropic arm, has provided US$1 million in funding to AI Singapore's Project Aquarium. This open data platform for Southeast Asia's languages will help improve the quality and availability of regional datasets, enabling developers to create culturally relevant AI solutions.

The company also launched an AI Centre of Excellence for Security, which will conduct research to tackle emerging threats in the agentic AI space while advancing security, privacy, and safety on mobile platforms.

Singapore's AI Ecosystem Gets a Boost

Since 2020, Google's upskilling programmes have helped nearly 350,000 Singaporeans develop new digital skills. The latest investments represent Google's most ambitious commitment yet to Singapore's AI ecosystem, combining talent development, research infrastructure, and industry partnerships.

With this expansion, Singapore solidifies its position as Southeast Asia's premier AI hub, attracting global technology companies and nurturing homegrown talent. As Google scales its operations in the city-state, the ripple effects are expected to benefit startups, enterprises, and workers across the entire region.
