CogniSense AI: Singapore's Edge Computing Pioneer

Date: February 2026

While much of the AI spotlight has focused on generative AI and large language models, a growing number of Singapore startups are carving out niches in specialised AI applications. CogniSense AI is one such company—a homegrown venture that is making significant strides in edge computing and artificial intelligence for IoT applications.

Bridging Cloud and Edge

Founded by a team of engineers from the National University of Singapore and Nanyang Technological University, CogniSense AI has developed proprietary algorithms that enable AI processing to occur directly on devices rather than relying solely on cloud infrastructure. This approach, known as edge AI, offers significant advantages in terms of latency, privacy, and reliability.

"Traditional AI systems send data to the cloud for processing, which introduces delays and raises bandwidth and privacy concerns," explained Dr. Sarah Chen, CogniSense's co-founder and chief technology officer. "Our technology allows AI to think and act locally, in real-time, which is essential for applications where every millisecond counts."

Smart City Applications

The company's solutions have found particular traction in smart city deployments. Singapore's ongoing efforts to create a more intelligent urban environment have created opportunities for technologies that can process data from thousands of sensors and cameras distributed across the city.

CogniSense AI's traffic management system, deployed in several districts, can analyse video feeds from traffic cameras to optimise signal timing in real-time. The system has reportedly reduced average commute times by up to 15 percent in pilot areas. Similarly, its environmental monitoring solutions can detect anomalies in air quality, noise levels, and other indicators, enabling rapid responses to developing situations.

Industrial IoT Transformation

Beyond smart cities, CogniSense AI has made significant inroads into industrial applications. Manufacturing facilities across Singapore and the wider region are adopting its predictive maintenance solutions, which can identify potential equipment failures before they occur.

The company's AI-powered quality inspection systems use computer vision to identify defects in real-time during production, helping manufacturers improve yields and reduce waste. This has proven particularly valuable in precision manufacturing sectors where quality standards are stringent.

Singapore's AI Ecosystem

CogniSense AI's growth reflects the broader maturation of Singapore's AI startup ecosystem. The city-state's combination of strong research institutions, government support through initiatives like AI Singapore, and access to regional markets has created fertile ground for AI ventures.

As edge computing continues to gain importance across industries—from autonomous vehicles to healthcare devices—companies like CogniSense AI are well-positioned to play a leading role in shaping the future of distributed intelligence.
