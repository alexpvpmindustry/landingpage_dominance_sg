Ocean Network Express and MTI Launch AI Joint Venture to Transform Shipping

Date: March 6, 2026

Singapore's maritime industry is set for a significant transformation with the announcement of a new joint venture between Ocean Network Express (ONE), one of the world's largest container shipping lines, and MTI, a leading maritime technology and investment firm. The partnership aims to accelerate the adoption of artificial intelligence across the shipping industry's operations and business models.

A Strategic Partnership for Maritime Innovation

Ocean Network Express, headquartered in Singapore, operates a fleet of over 250 container vessels serving major trade routes worldwide. MTI, also based in Singapore, brings deep expertise in maritime technology and digital transformation solutions. Together, they form a powerful alliance positioned to reshape how shipping companies leverage AI.

The joint venture will focus on developing and deploying AI-powered solutions across several key areas of maritime operations, including route optimization, predictive maintenance, cargo management, and port coordination. By harnessing the vast amounts of data generated by global shipping operations, the partners aim to achieve unprecedented levels of efficiency and cost reduction.

Why AI Matters for Shipping

The shipping industry handles over 90% of global trade by volume, yet it has historically been slow to adopt advanced digital technologies. Traditional operations rely heavily on manual processes, legacy systems, and human decision-making that can be slow and prone to error. AI offers the potential to revolutionize these operations in several ways.

Route optimization powered by machine learning algorithms can analyze weather patterns, ocean currents, fuel prices, and port congestion to identify the most efficient paths, potentially saving millions in fuel costs and reducing environmental impact. Predictive maintenance AI can anticipate equipment failures before they occur, preventing costly delays and improving safety.

For Singapore, a global maritime hub handling one-seventh of the world's container throughput, this joint venture represents a significant vote of confidence in the city-state's ability to lead maritime AI innovation. The partnership is expected to create new job opportunities for Singaporeans in AI development, data science, and maritime technology.

Singapore's Position as a Maritime AI Hub

This announcement reinforces Singapore's status as a center for both maritime excellence and AI innovation. The Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore (MPA) has been actively promoting digital transformation in the maritime sector, supporting initiatives that leverage AI, blockchain, and Internet of Things technologies.

Singapore's strategic location, world-class port infrastructure, and pro-business environment make it an ideal testing ground for maritime AI solutions. The ONE-MTI joint venture is expected to benefit from Singapore's robust ecosystem of technology companies, research institutions, and talent pool.

The partnership also aligns with Singapore's broader Smart Nation initiative, which aims to harness technology to improve lives and create economic opportunities. By bringing AI to one of the world's oldest industries, the joint venture demonstrates how emerging technologies can transform traditional sectors.

What This Means for Global Trade

The implications of this joint venture extend far beyond Singapore. With ONE operating globally and serving customers across Asia, Europe, and the Americas, AI solutions developed through this partnership have the potential to improve shipping efficiency worldwide. More efficient shipping means lower costs for consumers and businesses that rely on imported goods.

Environmental benefits are also significant. The shipping industry accounts for about 3% of global greenhouse gas emissions, and AI-driven optimization can help reduce fuel consumption and emissions. As global supply chains increasingly prioritize sustainability, AI-powered shipping solutions will become essential.

Looking Ahead

The joint venture is expected to begin operations in the second quarter of 2026, with initial pilot projects planned for routes serving Singapore's port. The partners have indicated plans to expand their AI solutions to other shipping routes and customers over time.

For Singaporeans interested in careers at the intersection of AI and maritime industry, this partnership opens exciting opportunities. The demand for AI professionals with domain expertise in logistics and shipping is expected to grow significantly in the coming years.

As the shipping industry navigates an era of increasing complexity—from supply chain disruptions to environmental regulations—AI-powered solutions offer a path forward. The ONE-MTI joint venture represents a significant milestone in that journey, positioning Singapore at the forefront of maritime AI innovation.
