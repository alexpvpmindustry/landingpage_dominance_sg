Budget 2026: Singapore's New SME AI Skills Launchpad to Accelerate AI Adoption

Date: March 3, 2026

In a landmark move to democratize artificial intelligence across Singapore's business landscape, the Ministry of Trade and Industry (MTI) has unveiled the SME AI Skills Launchpad as part of Budget 2026. This new initiative aims to help small and medium enterprises (SMEs) overcome the barriers to AI adoption and embrace digital transformation.

Bridging the AI Gap for Singapore's SMEs

Despite Singapore's reputation as a global AI hub, many local SMEs have lagged behind in adopting AI technologies. High costs, lack of technical expertise, and uncertainty about return on investment have prevented countless small businesses from leveraging AI's transformative potential.

The new SME AI Skills Launchpad addresses these challenges head-on by providing comprehensive support including government subsidies covering up to 70% of AI implementation costs, structured training programmes for employees, and access to pre-vetted AI solution providers who understand the unique needs of small businesses.

What's Included in the Launchpad

The programme offers a three-tier approach to AI adoption. First, awareness workshops will help business owners understand how AI can benefit their specific industry and operations. Second, capability-building courses will train employees to use AI tools effectively, from customer service chatbots to inventory management systems. Third, implementation support will connect SMEs with certified AI vendors who can deploy customized solutions at affordable rates.

Minister for Trade and Industry Chee Hong Tat emphasized the importance of inclusive AI adoption: "We want every Singaporean business, regardless of size, to benefit from the AI revolution. The SME AI Skills Launchpad is our commitment to ensuring no one gets left behind."

Industry Response and Expected Impact

Business associations have welcomed the initiative with enthusiasm. The Singapore Business Federation noted that many members have been eager to adopt AI but lacked the resources and guidance to do so confidently. The launchpad provides a structured pathway that reduces risk and uncertainty for cautious business owners.

Industry analysts predict the programme could benefit over 10,000 SMEs in its first year, potentially adding billions of dollars to Singapore's economy through improved productivity and new revenue opportunities. The initiative aligns with Singapore's broader goal of becoming a global AI leader while ensuring the benefits are widely shared across the business community.

Looking Ahead

Applications for the SME AI Skills Launchpad will open in April 2026, with the first cohort of participating businesses expected to begin their AI journey by mid-year. The government has allocated an initial fund of $200 million for the programme, with plans to expand based on demand and outcomes.

As Singapore continues to position itself at the forefront of AI innovation, the SME AI Skills Launchpad represents a crucial step in ensuring that the AI revolution benefits businesses of all sizes. For Singapore's vibrant SME sector, the future looks increasingly intelligent.
