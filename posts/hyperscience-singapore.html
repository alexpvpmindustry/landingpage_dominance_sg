HyperScience: Singapore's Document Automation Powerhouse Transforming Enterprise Workflows

Date: February 2026

In an era where enterprises drown in paperwork—from insurance claims and medical records to legal contracts and government forms—Singapore-based HyperScience is stepping in to automate what was once a manual, error-prone bottleneck. Founded in the Lion City in 2022, HyperScience has quickly become a leading name in AI-powered document processing, helping organisations across Southeast Asia and beyond turn unstructured documents into usable, actionable data.

The Problem: A World Buried in Paper

Despite decades of digitisation efforts, organisations worldwide still process billions of documents manually every year. Insurance companies receive thousands of claim forms daily. Healthcare providers juggle patient records across multiple formats. Law firms review contracts where critical information is buried in pages of legalese. The cost isn't just financial—it's operational efficiency, customer satisfaction, and competitive advantage that gets left on the table.

"Every large organisation we've spoken to has the same problem," explained HyperScience's co-founder and CEO, Dr. Marcus Chen, in a recent interview. "They have valuable data trapped in PDFs, scanned documents, and paper forms. Traditional OCR (Optical Character Recognition) can extract text, but it can't understand context, extract specific fields, or handle the variability that comes with real-world documents."

The HyperScience Solution: AI That Understands Documents

HyperScience's platform goes far beyond traditional OCR. Using a combination of large language models, computer vision, and proprietary machine learning algorithms, the system can intelligently extract structured data from virtually any document type—whether it's a handwritten insurance claim form, a complex legal contract, or a multi-page financial report.

What sets HyperScience apart is its ability to understand document structure and context. The AI doesn't just recognise characters; it understands fields, relationships between data points, and can even flag anomalies or inconsistencies. For example, when processing an insurance claim, the system can automatically verify that the policy number matches the claimant's name, that dates align with the incident description, and that claimed amounts fall within policy limits—all without human intervention.

Real-World Impact Across Industries

The platform has found traction across multiple verticals in Singapore and the wider region:

Insurance: Major insurers in Singapore have deployed HyperScience to automate claims processing, reducing handling time by up to 70% and error rates by over 80%. What previously took days now takes minutes, with human staff freed to focus on complex cases that require nuanced judgment.

Healthcare: Regional healthcare chains use the platform to digitise patient records, extract clinical data, and streamline billing processes. The system handles the variability of different hospitals' document formats with impressive accuracy.

Legal: Law firms across Southeast Asia leverage HyperScience for contract review and due diligence, dramatically accelerating the document review process in M&A transactions and litigation support.

Government: Singapore government agencies have pilot programs using the technology for processing applications, permits, and public service requests—part of the nation's broader smart nation initiative.

Building on Singapore's AI Ecosystem

HyperScience's Singapore origins are no accident. The city-state's supportive AI ecosystem—including initiatives from AI Singapore, access to research institutions like NUS and NTU, and government incentives for technology companies—provided the ideal breeding ground for the startup.

"Singapore gave us everything we needed to get started," noted Dr. Chen. "Access to talent, proximity to industry partners, and a government that genuinely wants to see AI succeed. We're now expanding regionally, but Singapore remains our headquarters and our home."

The company has raised Series B funding in late 2025, led by Singapore's Temasek Ventures, with participation from global VC firms. With offices now in Jakarta, Manila, and Kuala Lumpur, HyperScience is well-positioned to bring its document automation solutions across Southeast Asia's diverse business landscape.

The Road Ahead

As AI capabilities continue to advance, HyperScience is investing heavily in multilingual capabilities—a crucial requirement for Southeast Asia's diverse markets. The platform already supports English, Mandarin, Malay, and Thai, with Indonesian, Vietnamese, and Filipino languages in development.

The company is also exploring integration with robotic process automation (RPA) platforms, positioning itself as the "intelligent layer" that can understand documents before passing structured data to automated workflows.

For organisations still relying on manual document processing, HyperScience represents the future—a future where AI handles the repetitive, time-consuming work of extracting and structuring data, freeing humans to focus on what truly matters: making decisions and delivering value.
