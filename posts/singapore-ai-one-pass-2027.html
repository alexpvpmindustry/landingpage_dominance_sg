Singapore's New AI One Pass: Attracting Global Tech Talent in 2027

Date: March 5, 2026

Singapore is sharpening its appeal to the world's top AI and technology professionals with the launch of a new dedicated track under its Overseas Networks and Expertise (One) Pass. Announced during the Committee of Supply debate on March 3, 2026, the One Pass (AI and Tech) will replace the existing Tech.Pass scheme starting January 2027, bringing with it more attractive terms designed to lure the brightest minds in artificial intelligence and technology to the city-state.

A Strategic Upgrade to Talent Attraction

The move represents Singapore's strategic response to intensifying global competition for AI talent. With nations and corporations worldwide racing to build competitive advantages in artificial intelligence, Singapore recognizes that attracting top-tier AI professionals is essential to maintaining its position as a technology hub.

Manpower Minister Tan See Leng outlined the vision during his ministry's debate, describing the new track as targeting "movers and shakers, rainmakers and network brokers"—the kind of high-impact talent capable of spawning multiple enterprises and elevating Singapore's entire tech ecosystem.

The government expects these individuals to bring more than just their skills—they will transfer cutting-edge technology, thought processes, and expertise to the local workforce, creating ripple effects throughout Singapore's digital economy.

Flexible Compensation: Embracing Equity Culture

One of the most significant innovations in the One Pass (AI and Tech) track is its recognition of how top AI talent is actually compensated. Unlike traditional work passes that focus solely on cash salary, the new track allows applicants to meet the qualifying threshold using a mix of cash and equity-based remuneration.

This approach acknowledges the reality that leading AI professionals often receive substantial portions of their compensation through employee stock option plans, share ownership, and other equity-based instruments—components that were previously difficult to count toward visa qualification.

To qualify, applicants must still meet a monthly salary of at least S$30,000, either during the 12 months leading up to their application or from a prospective Singapore-based employer. However, this can now include stock-based compensation, dramatically expanding the pool of eligible talent.

Beyond Compensation: Ecosystem Appeal

Minister Tan emphasized that Singapore's value proposition extends beyond financial packages. He cited the Republic's reputation for trust, stability, and transparency as key differentiators in an uncertain world. These factors, combined with Singapore's established position as a global hub, position the city-state uniquely to attract talent seeking both career opportunity and quality of life.

The One Pass itself—introduced in 2023—allows holders flexibility to concurrently start, operate, and work for multiple companies, a feature particularly attractive to entrepreneurs and investors who want to diversify their activities without applying for multiple work passes.

Building on Success

The existing Tech.Pass, launched in 2021, targeted top-tier technology professionals and allowed holders to move seamlessly across activities—from running a business and investing, to mentoring or leading companies, as well as lecturing in institutes of higher learning.

The One Pass has seen healthy growth, with more than 8,000 individuals currently holding passes, many contributing to sectors critical to Singapore's future economy. The new AI and Tech track builds on this foundation while adding specialized appeal for the artificial intelligence sector.

Regional Implications

Singapore's aggressive talent acquisition strategy comes at a time when Southeast Asia is working to close its AI adoption gap. By attracting top AI talent, Singapore aims to position itself not just as a consumer of AI technology but as a creator and innovator—a hub where the next generation of AI breakthroughs could emerge.

For businesses operating in Singapore, the influx of high-caliber AI talent could accelerate digital transformation initiatives, foster new startup ecosystems, and strengthen the nation's competitive position in the global AI race.

As the January 2027 launch date approaches, all eyes will be on how the new One Pass (AI and Tech) performs in attracting the world's best AI minds to Singapore's shores.
