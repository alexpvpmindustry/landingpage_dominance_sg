TrustGuard AI: Singapore's Financial Crime Fighter Secures S$25M to Revolutionize Anti-Money Laundering

Published: April 10, 2026 | Category: Fintech AI

Singapore's fintech ecosystem continues to strengthen its position as Asia-Pacific's leading financial technology hub with the latest milestone from TrustGuard AI. The homegrown startup has secured S$25 million in Series B funding to expand its AI-powered anti-money laundering (AML) platform across the region, marking one of the largest investment rounds in Singapore's RegTech sector this year.

The Growing Challenge of Financial Crime

Financial institutions across Asia-Pacific face an escalating battle against money laundering and terrorist financing. According to the Basel Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Index, the Asia-Pacific region recorded over 2,300 high-risk AML incidents in 2025, with Singapore's banks processing increasingly complex cross-border transactions that require sophisticated detection capabilities.

Traditional rule-based AML systems, while effective to an extent, struggle to keep pace with evolving criminal tactics. Financial institutions generate thousands of alerts daily, overwhelming compliance teams and leading to high false-positive rates that consume significant resources.

TrustGuard AI's Intelligent Approach

Founded in 2023 by former MAS (Monetary Authority of Singapore) regulatory technology specialists, TrustGuard AI has developed a machine learning platform that analyzes transaction patterns, customer behavior, and network relationships to identify suspicious activities with unprecedented accuracy.

"Our AI doesn't just look at individual transactions—it understands the entire financial ecosystem," explained CEO Rebecca Tan. "By analyzing thousands of data points simultaneously, we've reduced false positive alerts by 73% while improving detection rates for genuine threats."

Funding and Expansion Plans

The Series B round was led by Singapore's Vertex Ventures with participation from global financial services giant Stripe and regional banking groups. The investment will support TrustGuard AI's expansion into Indonesia, Thailand, and Vietnam—markets where AML compliance requirements are tightening rapidly.

"Singapore continues to lead in RegTech innovation, and TrustGuard AI exemplifies how AI can strengthen financial system integrity while reducing compliance burden," said a spokesperson from Vertex Ventures.

Singapore's RegTech Ecosystem Thrives

The funding news underscores Singapore's growing influence in the RegTech sector. Following the MAS Fintech Awards and the launch of the Financial Services Transformation Programme, the city-state has positioned itself as a sandbox for innovative AML solutions.

TrustGuard AI currently serves over 40 financial institutions across Singapore, Malaysia, and Hong Kong, including three of Singapore's largest banks. The company plans to double its 80-member team by the end of 2026, with a focus on hiring machine learning engineers and compliance consultants.

Looking Ahead

With regulatory scrutiny intensifying across the region following the latest FATF (Financial Action Task Force) evaluation, demand for AI-powered compliance solutions is expected to surge. TrustGuard AI's successful funding round signals strong investor confidence in Singapore's RegTech capabilities.

The startup has also announced plans to integrate generative AI capabilities into its platform by Q3 2026, enabling compliance officers to query the system in natural language and receive instant insights into complex financial networks.

As financial crimes become more sophisticated, Singapore's AI-powered solutions like TrustGuard AI are proving that innovation and regulation can work hand in hand to protect the integrity of the financial system.
