AskData AI: Singapore's Natural Language Data Analytics Revolution

Date: March 8, 2026

In a world where data is often called the new oil, the ability to extract meaningful insights quickly has become a critical competitive advantage. Enter AskData AI, a Singapore-based startup that's transforming how enterprises interact with their data by allowing users to ask questions in plain English and receive instant, actionable insights.

Bridging the Gap Between Data and Decision-Makers

Traditionally, extracting insights from business data required specialized skills in SQL, Python, or business intelligence tools like Tableau and Power BI. This created a significant bottleneck, where non-technical decision-makers had to rely on data teams to answer even simple questions about their business performance.

AskData AI eliminates this barrier by employing advanced large language models specifically fine-tuned for enterprise data contexts. Users can simply type questions like "What were our top-selling products last quarter compared to the same period last year?" or "Which customer segments showed the highest churn risk in January?" and receive instant answers with supporting visualizations.

How It Works

The platform connects directly to an organization's existing data sources—whether it's Snowflake, Google BigQuery, Amazon Redshift, or traditional databases. Its AI engine understands the schema and relationships within these databases, allowing it to construct accurate queries on the fly.

"Our mission is to democratize data access," said Dr. Sarah Chen, AskData AI's founder and former data science lead at DBS Bank. "Every employee should be able to make data-driven decisions without waiting in queue for a data analyst."

Enterprise-Grade Security and Governance

Recognizing that enterprises cannot compromise on data security, AskData AI has built robust governance features. The platform supports role-based access controls, ensuring users can only query data they're authorized to see. All queries are logged for audit purposes, and the system includes PII detection to prevent accidental exposure of sensitive customer information.

The company has also achieved SOC 2 Type II certification and complies with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), making it suitable for deployment in regulated industries like banking and healthcare.

Growing Traction in Singapore and Beyond

Since its founding in late 2024, AskData AI has secured partnerships with several prominent Singapore-based organizations, including logistics giant Ninja Van, healthcare provider Raffles Medical Group, and real estate developer CapitaLand.

The company recently closed a $12 million Series A funding round led by Sequoia Capital India, with participation from existing investors Golden Gate Ventures and Wavemaker Partners. The funding will be used to expand its engineering team in Singapore and accelerate go-to-market efforts in Southeast Asia.

"Singapore is the perfect launchpad for us," explained Dr. Chen. "The government strongly supports AI innovation, there's a deep talent pool, and companies here are forward-thinking about digital transformation."

The Competitive Landscape

AskData AI enters a competitive market that includes global players like Microsoft's Copilot for Business Intelligence and ThoughtSpot's AI-powered analytics. However, the company believes its focus on the Southeast Asian market and deep integration with regional data platforms gives it an edge.

Industry analysts note that the natural language analytics market in Southeast Asia is expected to grow at a compound annual growth rate of 34% through 2030, driven by increasing digitalization and a growing shortage of skilled data professionals.

What's Next

Looking ahead, AskData AI plans to expand its language capabilities to support Mandarin, Malay, and Tamil—reflecting Singapore's multilingual workforce. The company is also developing voice-activated queries, allowing users to ask questions during meetings and receive instant answers through smart speakers or video conferencing integrations.

As organizations increasingly recognize that competitive advantage lies in speed of decision-making, tools like AskData AI that remove friction from data access are poised for significant growth. For Singapore's businesses, the ability to get answers from data as easily as asking a colleague could be a game-changer.
