STT GDC and SuperX Launch AI Innovation Centre in Singapore to Fast-Track Enterprise AI

Date: April 15, 2026

Singapore continues to cement its position as Asia's AI hub with the launch of a new AI Innovation Centre right in the heart of the city. ST Telemedia Global Data Centres (STT GDC), a Temasek-backed data centre provider, has partnered with SuperX AI Technology to open a dedicated facility aimed at helping enterprises accelerate their artificial intelligence initiatives from experimental stages to production-ready scale.

A Launch Pad for Enterprise AI

The AI Innovation Centre is hosted at the STT Singapore 5 facility in Tai Seng, and marks a significant step forward in Singapore's ambition to become a leading AI destination in Asia. The centre serves as what partners call a "dedicated environment" for enterprises looking to test, develop, and deploy AI solutions at scale.

Already, the centre has onboarded an initial cohort of users, with early adopters leveraging its high-performance compute capabilities for complex workloads including advanced modelling and large-scale data simulations. This rapid adoption signals strong market demand for accessible AI infrastructure in the region.

Nvidia Blackwell GPUs at the Core

At the heart of the new centre lies direct access to the latest Nvidia Blackwell graphics processing unit (GPU) infrastructure—a powerhouse configuration featuring high-speed video random-access memory of 192 gigabytes per GPU. This represents some of the most capable compute resources available for AI workloads today.

The significance of this offering cannot be overstated. As AI models grow in complexity and enterprises demand more sophisticated capabilities, access to cutting-edge GPU infrastructure has become a critical bottleneck. By providing direct access to Blackwell-class GPUs, the centre removes a major barrier for companies looking to scale their AI initiatives.

"The foundation of any successful AI strategy is dependable, scalable infrastructure," said Lim Mingcheng, Country Head of Singapore at STT GDC. "This centre is more than just a testing ground; it is a launch pad for enterprises to achieve measurable return on investment and long-term AI leadership."

Pilot Programme Lowers the Barrier to Entry

Perhaps the most compelling aspect of the new centre is its pilot access programme, which offers qualified customers a 14-day free trial. This initiative allows enterprises to validate performance, compatibility, and cost-efficiency before committing to full-scale deployment—a crucial consideration for organizations still experimenting with AI strategies.

All workloads run locally in Singapore within what the partners describe as a "secure, resilient environment," meeting the data-residency requirements of regulated industries. This is particularly important for financial services, healthcare, and government sectors that handle sensitive data and face strict regulatory requirements around data sovereignty.

Partnership Builds on Shared Vision

The collaboration between STT GDC and SuperX was formalized in January 2026 when both parties signed a memorandum of understanding. The AI Innovation Centre is now open to enterprises, regional businesses, and institutions of higher learning—signalling broader accessibility to Singapore's growing AI ecosystem.

STT GDC, headquartered in Singapore, operates data centres across multiple markets including Thailand, Japan, and Germany. SuperX brings expertise as an infrastructure solutions provider specifically focused on AI data centres. Together, they bring complementary strengths to this venture.

What This Means for Singapore's AI Ecosystem

The launch of this AI Innovation Centre reflects Singapore's strategic push to attract AI-related investments and talent. By providing accessible infrastructure with enterprise-grade security and performance, the centre lowers barriers to entry for companies looking to experiment with AI—whether startups testing their first models or established enterprises piloting new use cases.
