Ocean Network Express and MTI Join Forces to Bring AI to Global Shipping

Date: February 24, 2026

In a landmark move that underscores the shipping industry's accelerating digital transformation, Ocean Network Express (ONE), one of the world's largest container shipping lines, has announced a joint venture with Singapore-based MTI to advance artificial intelligence adoption across the maritime sector. The partnership aims to revolutionize operations and business models using cutting-edge AI technologies.

Bridging Traditional Shipping and Modern AI

The shipping industry, long considered traditional and slow to adopt new technologies, is experiencing a significant shift. Ocean Network Express, headquartered in Singapore, operates a fleet of over 200 container vessels serving major trade routes globally. By partnering with MTI, the company is signaling its commitment to leveraging AI for competitive advantage in an increasingly digital world.

"This joint venture represents a pivotal moment for the shipping industry," said a spokesperson from Ocean Network Express. "We are committed to leading the digital transformation of maritime operations, and AI will be at the core of this evolution. From optimizing route planning to predictive maintenance and cargo management, artificial intelligence will help us deliver better service to our customers while improving operational efficiency."

Singapore's Role as a Maritime AI Hub

The partnership highlights Singapore's growing prominence as a hub for maritime technology and innovation. As one of the world's busiest port cities, Singapore has long been at the forefront of shipping and logistics innovation. The city-state's strategic location, robust digital infrastructure, and government support for technology adoption make it an ideal testing ground for AI solutions in the maritime sector.

Industry analysts note that this joint venture could serve as a catalyst for broader AI adoption across Southeast Asia's shipping industry. With the region's ports handling over a quarter of global container traffic, the potential impact of AI-powered optimization could be substantial.

AI Applications in Shipping

The joint venture is expected to explore AI applications across multiple areas of shipping operations:

  • Route Optimization: AI algorithms can analyze weather patterns, traffic congestion, and fuel prices to determine the most efficient routes, potentially reducing costs and emissions significantly.
  • Predictive Maintenance: Machine learning models can predict equipment failures before they occur, reducing downtime and maintenance costs for vessel operators.
  • Cargo Management: AI-powered systems can optimize container stuffing and stowage, maximizing space utilization and reducing port handling times.
  • Demand Forecasting: Advanced analytics can predict shipping demand patterns, helping companies optimize fleet deployment and pricing strategies.
  • Port Operations: AI can streamline port operations, from automated berthing to intelligent container yard management.

Economic and Environmental Implications

The shipping industry accounts for approximately 3% of global greenhouse gas emissions, and there is increasing pressure to reduce environmental impact. AI-powered optimization could play a significant role in this effort by reducing fuel consumption through better route planning and more efficient operations.

Beyond environmental benefits, the economic implications are substantial. The global shipping industry moves over 80% of world trade by volume, and even modest efficiency improvements can translate into billions of dollars in savings. Industry experts estimate that AI optimization could reduce operational costs by 10-15% over the next decade.

Looking Ahead

As the joint venture between Ocean Network Express and MTI moves forward, it represents a significant vote of confidence in AI's potential to transform the shipping industry. For Singapore, this partnership reinforces the city-state's position as a leader in maritime technology innovation.

The shipping industry stands at an inflection point. With the adoption of AI technologies accelerating across the sector, we can expect to see more partnerships like this one as companies seek to gain competitive advantages in an increasingly digital marketplace. The question is no longer whether AI will transform shipping, but how quickly the transformation will happen.
