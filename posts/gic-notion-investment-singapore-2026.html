GIC Invests in Notion: Singapore Sovereign Wealth Fund Backs AI Workspace Platform

Date: March 15, 2026

Singapore's sovereign wealth fund GIC has made its first investment in Notion, the popular AI-powered workspace platform, participating in a landmark US$270 million employee share sale that values the company at US$11 billion. This strategic investment marks a significant vote of confidence in the AI-powered productivity tools sector and underscores Singapore's growing role as a global tech investor.

A Landmark Investment for Singapore's Tech Ambitions

The investment, announced in late January 2026, saw GIC join existing investors Sequoia Capital and Index Ventures in the tender offer. The shares were sold by current and former Notion employees, making it one of the largest secondary market transactions in the productivity software space this year.

Notion identified GIC as an "obvious choice" as an investment partner, citing the sovereign wealth fund's extensive expertise in supporting growth across Asia-Pacific and its long-term investment horizon. "One of the reasons we're so thrilled to partner with GIC is because of their expertise in supporting growth in Apac and across the globe, as well as their commitment to the long term. They think in decades, not quarters," the company stated.

Notion's Singapore Connection

The GIC investment comes on the heels of Notion's expansion into Singapore, with the company opening a Singapore office in mid-2025 as part of its broader push for regional expansion. The Singapore hub joins other Asia-Pacific offices in Tokyo, Seoul, and Sydney, positioning Notion to capture the rapidly growing demand for AI-powered workspace tools in the region.

Notion co-founder Akshay Kothari highlighted the company's "explosive growth" in Asia-Pacific during a LinkedIn post announcing the tender offer. This growth trajectory clearly influenced GIC's decision to participate in the investment round, signaling the sovereign wealth fund's belief in the region's AI adoption potential.

AI Driving Notion's Revenue Transformation

What makes this investment particularly noteworthy is Notion's rapid transformation into an AI-powered platform. The company revealed that more than 50% of its annual recurring revenue now comes from AI-enabled customers—a figure that has more than doubled over the past year. This explosive adoption of AI features within the Notion platform demonstrates how traditional productivity tools are evolving to meet the demands of an AI-first workplace.

Notion's platform offers highly customizable functions that combine note-taking, databases, and project management onto a single platform. Businesses and individuals use it to streamline operations related to goal setting, status tracking, and task management—all areas where AI assistants are increasingly adding value through automation and intelligence.

Empowering Employees Through Equity

As part of the transaction, Notion took significant steps to benefit its employees. The company removed the one-year vesting cliff on options, allowing nearly all Notion employees to participate in the tender offer. This move reflects a growing trend in tech companies offering greater flexibility to employees in monetizing their equity, particularly in high-growth AI companies.

What This Means for Singapore's Tech Ecosystem

GIC's investment in Notion represents more than just a financial transaction—it signals Singapore's ambition to be at the center of the global AI revolution. By backing a company that has established a presence in Singapore, GIC is helping to attract more AI-focused companies to the city-state while providing capital to firms that are building the productivity tools of tomorrow.

The investment also highlights how Singapore's sovereign wealth funds are increasingly active in the technology sector, particularly in companies that demonstrate strong AI capabilities and regional growth potential. As more enterprises adopt AI-powered tools for workplace productivity, Singapore's investment in this space positions the city-state to benefit from the technological transformation sweeping across industries.

With Notion's Singapore office now operational and the backing of GIC, the AI workspace company is well-positioned to accelerate its growth across Southeast Asia—a region where enterprise adoption of AI tools is expected to surge in the coming years.
