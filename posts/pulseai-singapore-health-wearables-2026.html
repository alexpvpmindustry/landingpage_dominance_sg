Pulse AI: Singapore's Health Wearable Revolution with Real-Time AI Diagnostics

Date: April 14, 2026

Singapore continues to position itself as Southeast Asia's health tech hub, and Pulse AI is leading the charge in AI-powered wearable technology. The Singapore startup has developed a revolutionary health wearable that provides real-time diagnostic insights, potentially transforming preventive healthcare across the region.

Bridging Healthcare Gaps with AI

Founded by a team of NUS alumni and healthcare professionals in 2024, Pulse AI addresses a critical need in Singapore's aging society: making continuous health monitoring accessible and affordable. Their flagship device, the PulseBand, goes beyond traditional fitness trackers to offer clinical-grade health insights.

"Traditional healthcare is reactive — you visit a doctor when something feels wrong," explained Dr. Sarah Chen, Pulse AI's co-founder and former KK Women's and Children's Hospital physician. "We're making healthcare predictive. Our AI can detect anomalies in vital signs days before they become serious health events."

How the Technology Works

The PulseBand wearable monitors over 20 biometric parameters including heart rate variability, blood oxygen levels, skin temperature, and even subtle electrochemical changes in the wearer's sweat. But the real magic lies in the embedded AI processor that analyzes this data in real-time.

The startup's proprietary algorithm, trained on millions of anonymized health records from Singapore's public hospitals, can identify patterns associated with early-stage cardiovascular issues, metabolic disturbances, and even emerging infectious diseases — all without connecting to cloud services, ensuring user privacy.

Singapore's Digital Health Ecosystem

Pulse AI's emergence reflects Singapore's growing strength in digital health. The city-state has actively fostered health tech innovation through initiatives like the Smart National Health IC, which enables secure health data sharing across the ecosystem.

The company has already partnered with Singapore's largest health insurance provider to offer the PulseBand as part of preventive care packages. Early pilot programs with corporate employees have shown promising results, with participating companies reporting a 23% reduction in health-related productivity losses.

Regional Expansion Plans

With S$12 million in Series A funding secured earlier this year, Pulse AI is now eyeing regional expansion. The startup plans to bring its AI wearables to Indonesia, Thailand, and Vietnam — markets where healthcare infrastructure is stretched but smartphone penetration is high.

"Our goal is to make preventive health monitoring accessible to 50 million Southeast Asians within the next three years," said Chen. "Singapore will serve as our R&D hub and proof-of-concept location."

Challenges Ahead

Despite the optimism, challenges remain. Regulatory hurdles differ across ASEAN markets, and Pulse AI must navigate varying medical device certification requirements. Additionally, convincing consumers to invest in health wearables requires demonstrating clear, tangible benefits.

The company is also addressing concerns about data privacy — a hot-button issue in healthcare. Pulse AI emphasizes that all AI processing happens on-device, and users have complete control over their health data.

What This Means for Singapore's AI Future

Pulse AI represents a new wave of Singapore AI companies focusing on vertical-specific applications rather than general-purpose AI. This specialization, combined with the city-state's robust regulatory framework and strong healthcare infrastructure, positions Singapore as a potential leader in health AI.

As Singapore continues to navigate the balance between innovation and regulation, startups like Pulse AI demonstrate that responsible AI development and commercial success aren't mutually exclusive. The Lion City's AI health tech scene is certainly one to watch.
